# Enzai

> Enzai is an enterprise AI governance platform that helps organizations adopt AI while managing regulatory and operational risk. Its product suite spans agentic AI governance, AI use-case intake and approval workflows, a centralized AI registry, and compliance frameworks that map AI initiatives to the EU AI Act, GDPR, NIST AI RMF, and ISO 42001. Belfast-based with a London office; ISO 27001 certified; backed by venture capital including Seedcamp. No public API or developer portal is published at this time.

## Products
- [Agentic AI Governance](https://enz.ai/product/agentic-ai-governance-draft): Real-time analysis, risk flagging, and agent-sprawl management
- [AI Use Cases & Initiatives](https://enz.ai/product/ai-use-cases-and-initiatives-draft): Structured intake and approval workflows for AI projects
- [AI Registry](https://enz.ai/product/ai-inventory-draft): Centralized inventory of first- and third-party AI systems
- [Compliance Frameworks](https://enz.ai/product/ai-compliance-frameworks-draft): Alignment with EU AI Act, GDPR, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001
